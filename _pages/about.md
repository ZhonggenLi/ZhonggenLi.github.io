I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the College of Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang University (ZJU), advised by Prof. <u>[Yunjun Gao](https://person.zju.edu.cn/gaoyj_cn)</u>. I received my B.E. degree from Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT) in June 2023, where I was advised by Prof. <u>[Hongzhi Wang](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/wang)</u> and Prof. <u>[Xiaoou Ding](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/dingxiaoou)</u>.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on **AI and data systems** through the lens of **software-hardware co-design**. Specifically, my current interests include:

**Data Infrastructure for AI:**
algorithms and systems for vector data management, memory-augmented AI systems, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), targeting both cloud and edge environments.

**Efficient LLM Systems:**
 efficient training and inference for large language models, and system-level optimization for agentic AI and embodied intelligence applications.

I am open to collaborations and internship opportunities in **AI infrastructure** and **efficient LLM training & inference**. Please feel free to contact me via [email](mailto:zglics@gmail.com).
